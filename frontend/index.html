HOW IT WORKS
ACT 01 · FORECAST

It learns each machine's rhythm.

Ninety days of sales for every machine and every product. A moving average, adjusted for the day of the week, because gyms spike on Saturdays and offices go quiet. Plain arithmetic you can check by hand.

HOW IT WORKS
ACT 02 · PROPOSE

It writes the order before you ask.

When something is on course to run out inside a week, Restock drafts the purchase order for you: quantities to reach fourteen days of cover, unit costs, one clean total.

HOW IT WORKS
ACT 03 · DECIDE

You approve it, or you don't.

Nothing is bought until the owner taps approve. Every approval lands in the audit log with a timestamp and a cap check, so the books always explain themselves.
