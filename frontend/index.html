Every empty coil is a sale you didn't make. Operators either drive routes to eyeball stock, or babysit spreadsheets that stop scaling at machine number three. Meanwhile the machines already know exactly what happened — nobody's listening.

ACT 01
FORECAST

It watches every column.

Ninety days of sales per machine, per SKU. A moving average tuned by day-of-week rhythm — gyms spike on Saturday, offices die on weekends. No black box, no "trust the model." You can read the math in one sitting.

ACT 02
PROPOSE

It drafts the order, not the invoice.

When a SKU is on course to stock out inside a week, Restock writes the purchase order — quantities to reach 14 days of cover, unit costs, one clean total. Ready before you knew there was a problem.

ACT 03
DECIDE

One tap. Or none.

Nothing ships until the owner says so. Approve in a tap, or walk away — the AI can propose all it wants, it holds no wallet. Every approval lands in the audit log, timestamped and cap-checked.

An AI with a leash.
